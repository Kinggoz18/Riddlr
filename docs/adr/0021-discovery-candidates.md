# Discovery candidates

Discovery is application-computed from clustered evidence. A cluster with
independent evidence can persist as a candidate without LLM analysis.
Reprints are not candidates. LLM analysis remains gated to material events.

A candidate is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or trade. Opportunity means
the item may warrant further investigation. Epistemic statuses stay
distinct: discovered, observed, confirmed, inferred, and signal.
