Purpose: ask whether something is starting. This is not narrative-detection.

Narrative-detection asks whether the thing is becoming a coherent, persistent market narrative. This skill asks only: is there evidence of emergence?

Look for first independent mentions, appearance on a second distinct source, acceleration from a low baseline, novel information, and early persistence.

A recycled headline is not a new trend. A mention spike from one source is insufficient.

Prohibited: treating velocity on one host as emergence; inventing novelty; claiming a narrative has formed.

Distinctions: first mention vs reprint; multi-source emergence vs single-channel noise.

Output: whether emergence is supported, what confirmation is still missing, and that the finding is an early trend candidate rather than a narrative.
