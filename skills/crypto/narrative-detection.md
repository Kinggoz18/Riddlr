Purpose: decide whether attention has become a coherent market narrative.

Framework, in order: isolated attention → acceleration → breadth → persistence → market confirmation → structural significance. A social spike is not a narrative. Mentions +500% on one host is isolated attention.

Evidence: independent hosts, source families, duration, recurrence, and sourced market reaction when present. Ask whether attention is concentrated in one community and whether the story is new or recycled.

Prohibited: treating reprint count as breadth; inventing volume, liquidity, or confirmation; granting trading authority.

Distinctions: isolated vs accelerating; one-source vs cross-source; persistent vs recycled; market-confirmed vs structurally significant.

Output: the highest stage the evidence supports, with low confidence and low materiality when breadth, persistence, or confirmation is missing.
