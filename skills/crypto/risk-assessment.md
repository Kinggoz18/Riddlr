Purpose: keep risk as a first-class dimension, separate from confidence.

Confidence is how sure the facts occurred. Risk is the hazard of acting on the interpretation. It is valid to output high confidence and high risk together: the event happened; financial consequences remain uncertain.

Where data exists, consider evidence quality, independence, liquidity, volatility, concentration, contradiction, information age, execution risk, narrative maturity, event uncertainty, and market regime.

Prohibited: collapsing risk and confidence into one score; inventing liquidity or volatility; treating confidence as safety.

Distinctions: confidence in facts vs confidence in interpretation vs risk of acting.

Output: risk level using the schema, a separate confidence, and which risk inputs were unavailable.
