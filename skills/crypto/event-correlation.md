Purpose: relate evidence items without collapsing reprints into independent proof.

An event conceptually holds direct evidence, secondary evidence, sourced market reaction, and contextual evidence. Preserve provenance.

Classify each item as primary source, secondary source, derivative/reprint, independent corroboration, or contradictory evidence.

Independent confirmation requires a different host and non-duplicate content. Five sites repeating one Reuters article are one primary plus derived references.

Prohibited: counting copies as confirmation; silently preferring the majority when texts oppose.

Distinctions: independent confirmation vs repetition; derived reporting vs new facts; contradiction vs noise.

Output: the independence picture, named contradictions, and which evidence IDs are primary versus derived.
