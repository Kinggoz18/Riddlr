Purpose: assess stablecoin stress from evidence, not from assumed reserves.

Where evidence actually exists, consider peg behaviour, liquidity, redemption pressure, supply changes, issuer actions, reserve disclosures, exchange flows, counterparty concerns, regulatory developments, and broader market stress.

Observed data must stay distinguishable from inference. Missing reserve, redemption, or flow figures stay missing.

Prohibited: inventing reserve values, redemption activity, issuer exposure, peg statistics, liquidity, or exchange flows.

Distinctions: peg mention vs measured peg; disclosure vs rumour; issuer action vs commentator claim.

Output: which risk dimensions are evidenced, which are unavailable, and a bounded interpretation that does not mint numbers.
