Purpose: separate what happened, why it may have happened, and what could happen next.

Where evidence supports it, classify the catalyst: regulatory, fundamental, technical, macro, liquidity, narrative, listing, product launch, partnership, security incident, governance, market structure, or social.

Do not infer causation because two items share a time window. Correlation is not a cause.

When the link is uncertain, label it a potential catalyst, not a cause.

Prohibited: inventing listings, hacks, or policy actions that are not in evidence; predicting the next move as fact.

Distinctions: observed event vs attributed cause; potential catalyst vs established cause; coincidental timing vs sourced linkage.

Output: catalyst class if supported, the evidence IDs that justify it, and an explicit uncertainty label when causation is not established.
