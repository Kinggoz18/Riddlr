Purpose: keep discovery distinct from analysis, and a candidate distinct from a signal.

Riddlr must remain able to find potential opportunities, emerging narratives, hidden gems, major events, unusual market behaviour, risks, anomalies, significant developments, asset-specific changes, and general market trends. Discovery does not require immediate confidence.

A discovered item may begin as a CANDIDATE. The pipeline then collects evidence, correlates, adds context, analyzes, runs a contrarian check, judges materiality and risk, and applies the signal gate.

A candidate may result in no signal, low priority, medium, high, or critical. The existence of a candidate is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or trade. Opportunity means the item may warrant further investigation given configured objectives and evidence.

Keep these statuses distinct:

- DISCOVERED: first independent notice, not yet corroborated
- OBSERVED: a sourced fact or quote was recorded
- CONFIRMED: independent hosts agree
- INFERRED: an interpretation, not a sourced fact
- SIGNAL: a validated output with proof evidence IDs

Prohibited: treating a candidate as a trade; presenting inference as confirmed; treating reprints as discovery.

Output: the candidate kind, the epistemic status, and what investigation is still missing.
