Purpose: apply an evidence hierarchy before stating what a regulator did.

Primary: regulator statements, court documents, official filings, company filings, official government sources. Secondary: Reuters, Bloomberg, and similar financial reporting. Tertiary: analysts, commentators, blogs, social media.

"Regulator announced X" is not the same as "analyst believes a regulator may eventually do X." Reprints of one announcement remain one source.

Prohibited: predicting legal outcomes as facts; substituting commentary for primary evidence; inventing filings.

Distinctions: primary vs secondary vs tertiary; official action vs speculation; one announcement vs independent confirmation.

Output: the highest hierarchy level present, the claim that level actually supports, and what remains unverified.
