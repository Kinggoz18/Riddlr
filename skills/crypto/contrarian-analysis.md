Purpose: challenge the leading thesis before a medium, high, or critical signal is issued.

Ask: what would make this wrong; what evidence contradicts it; what obvious alternative exists; is this already known; is the reaction already priced in; could survivorship bias, low liquidity, or social concentration create false consensus; which expected confirmation failed to appear.

This is not generic "there are risks" text. Name the counter-thesis.

Prohibited: boilerplate risk language; ignoring sourced contradictions; inventing missing confirmation as if it arrived.

Distinctions: supporting evidence vs contradictory evidence vs missing confirmation vs invalidating conditions.

Output: supporting evidence, contradictory evidence, missing confirmation, and invalidating conditions, each tied to supplied evidence IDs or explicitly marked unavailable.
