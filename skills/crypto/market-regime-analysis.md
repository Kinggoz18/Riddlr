Purpose: answer how the current market environment changes interpretation of this event.

The same event can matter differently in risk-on versus risk-off conditions, in a broad trend, under different liquidity or volatility, with Bitcoin context, or with sector correlation.

Use only sourced observations supplied by the application. If those facts are missing, state that the regime cannot be confidently assessed.

Prohibited: inventing risk-on/risk-off labels, volatility regimes, or BTC correlation; filling gaps from model memory.

Distinctions: sourced regime vs unavailable context; local asset move vs broad market move when both are sourced.

Output: the regime implication if data exists, otherwise an explicit unavailable statement. Do not let an unsupported regime change materiality.
